(~2.5 min) Thesis: Kaohsiung 高雄 is Taiwan's industrial port city; it matters to the world as a place where goods are made and shipped and, now, where the most advanced chips are produced. NUMBERS: about 9.2 million twenty-foot containers (TEU) in 2024 (Lloyd's List: 9,228,417); the export processing zone opened late 1966 and is generally called the world's first; TSMC's Fab 22 in Nanzih began 2-nanometre volume production in Q4 2025. PARTNERS. Europe: shipping lines; Danish Ørsted had wind-farm foundations built at Singda Harbor (China Steel subsidiary Sing Da Marine Structure); Danish NKT's cable joint venture with Walsin Lihwa. Japan: colonial government built up port and railway; Japanese firms supply chip materials and tools; banana and sugar exports in the past. US: American firms among early export-zone tenants; main buyer of chips; Hon Hai (Foxconn) and Nvidia announced an AI computing centre at the Kaohsiung Software Park. ASEAN: transshipment cargo, Singapore competition, migrant workers. China/HK: rival ports, big market, where many factories relocated. GLOBALIZATION. Gains: the export zone gave foreign firms tax breaks and rural migrants jobs; Ten Major Construction (1970s) built China Steel, a shipyard and refinery capacity; the port was in the world top three in the 1990s (sources vary: 1980s or 1992-99); today TSMC and chip-packaging group ASE (founded in Kaohsiung 1984). Costs: after the 1985-87 currency rise, labour-intensive factories relocated to China; the port fell to 6th in 2006 and around 18th in 2023 as Shanghai, Busan and Singapore gained; polluted rivers and air; on 31 July 2014 leaking propylene exploded in Qianzhen and Lingya: 32 dead, 321 injured; population fell from about 2.77 million to about 2.7 million as young people left. UNCERTAIN, verify: port 2024 rank; top-three years; 2023 rank (one source); banana and sugar exports; US firms in the export zone; Europe-Asia lines and migrant workers (general knowledge); population figures approximate.

(~2.5 min) TIMELINE (1.5 min). 1970s: export zone (1966) and Nantze zone (1969), then Ten Major Construction: China Steel (founded 1971, first furnace 1977), CSBC shipyard (1973), refinery; oil crises 1973/79 hit a refining and shipping economy. 1980s: after the 1985-87 currency rise, labour-intensive factories relocated to China and Southeast Asia. 1990s: port among the top three (sources vary: 1980s or 1992-99); pollution protests; ASE, founded in Kaohsiung in 1984, grew; Taiwan came through the 1997 Asian crisis well. 2000s: port fell to 6th in 2006 as Shanghai, Busan and Singapore gained; Love River cleaned (~2007), metro 2008; global financial crisis 2008-09 cut trade. 2010s-now: Asia New Bay Area (from 2011), 2014 gas explosions (32 dead, 321 injured), offshore-wind parts, TSMC Fab 22 in Nanzih (2nm volume production from Q4 2025). Pattern: shocks pushed the city up the value chain, but slowly. Overall shift: export-zone assembly, then heavy industry and port, then services, culture and advanced chips. NEW PATH (1 min): Yes, real: TSMC fab on former CPC refinery land (reported ~NT$1.5 trillion over several phases), ASE chip packaging, wind foundations and cables, Software Park, a cleaner city. But narrow: one anchor and its suppliers; steel, refining and petrochemicals still big; growth in a few districts (Nanzih, Qiaotou, New Bay Area); youth drain not clearly reversed. Call it early and fragile, not finished. Q4 · POLICY · PART 2 · ASSESSING DEVELOPMENT OUTCOMES

Policy, and who drove Kaohsiung's new paths

Industrial & spatial

Export zones (1966, 1969); Ten Major Construction (1970s); Southern Taiwan Science Park links; Asia New Bay Area (city, 2011); Nanzih chip park

Impact: industrial base, now a chip corridor

Regional revitalization

Local Revitalization (2019): fight depopulation, back local brands and youth return in rural districts

Impact: early stage; small next to big projects

1 · New paths?

Export zone, heavy industry, port, then culture, chips, wind. Old paths faded; new are early.

2 · Who led?

National government: zones, steel, ships. City hall: Love River, metro, New Bay Area. No EU-style body.

3 · What sparked it?

1966 zone plan; China Steel (1971); waterfront renewal; TSMC on old refinery land.

4 · Inside or outside?

Both: decisions from Taipei and outside firms; city and local firms carry out.

5 · Endogenous role

ASE, China Steel, city renewal, pollution protests, Software Park, start-up support.

6 · Market forces

Global: container, chip, wind demand. National: state firms, subsidies. Local: land, labour, port.

(~3 min) POLICY (1.5 min). Taiwan has no EU-style regional policy; a developmental state used spatial policy as industrial policy, and Kaohsiung is where much of it landed. National: export zone (1966) and Nantze zone (1969); Linhai Industrial Park and Ten Major Construction (China Steel, shipyard, refinery); Southern Taiwan Science Park (1996, based in Tainan, Kaohsiung campus at Qiaotou). City-level: Asia New Bay Area, about 600 hectares of former port land, begun 2011 (Exhibition Center 2013, Music Center, cruise terminal, Software Park; about NT$130 billion, one source); Nanzih Technology Industrial Park on former CPC refinery land, now hosting TSMC. Local Revitalization (2019, National Development Council) targets depopulating areas outside big cities; in Kaohsiung that means outer rural and mountain districts. ASSESSMENT (1.5 min), six questions in order. 1) Several paths in sequence: zone and heavy industry worked for decades, port peaked in the 1990s then lost rank, chips and wind promising but only a few years old. 2) National government via zones and state-linked firms (China Steel, CSBC, CPC); city government (directly elected mayors since the 1990s) led the river clean-up, metro, New Bay Area and courting TSMC; no EU-type body. 3) Sparks: 1966 plan, 1970s projects, 2011 New Bay Area, TSMC choosing Nanzih. 4) Mixed: major decisions came from the capital and firms based elsewhere (TSMC is in Hsinchu); Ørsted and NKT invested; locals executed. 5) Endogenous: ASE, China Steel, renewal projects, anti-pollution activism, Software Park, partnerships with universities. 6) Market forces at three scales. UNCERTAIN, verify: which districts are Local Revitalization targets; Software Park opening year (Wikipedia: 2000); NT$130 billion; 1996 date; 1990s Houjing anti-petrochemical protests (general knowledge); city fast-tracking TSMC permits; mayoral dates.

CONCLUSION

Conclusion: a hybrid development model

Inherited economic systems

Colonial-era port; state-owned steel, ships, refining; export-zone model.

Influence: Strong, double-edged

Long-standing social strengths

Skilled port and factory workforce, engineers, active citizens, local pride.

Influence: Moderate to strong

External support

US and Japanese firms and markets; European wind investors; global demand.

Influence: Decisive early, still key

State-guided: the government led

  • Export processing zone (1966), built by the government
  • China Steel, CSBC shipyard and refinery: Ten Major Construction
  • Nanzih chip park on state refinery land; city-led New Bay Area

Externally enabled: outside help and demand

  • US and other foreign firms in the export zone; US market
  • Denmark's Ørsted orders wind foundations; NKT cable plant
  • Hon Hai–Nvidia AI centre planned; global chip demand

Rooted locally: ASE, China Steel and active citizens made it last. Next: widen beyond one chip anchor and keep young people.

(~2 min) CONCLUSION (1 min). No single factor explains Kaohsiung's outcomes. Inherited systems (colonial-era port, state-owned heavy industry, export-zone model) set the base and also created today's pollution and dependence. Long-standing social strengths (skilled port and factory workforce, engineers behind ASE and China Steel, citizens who pushed for clean rivers and a new waterfront) kept the city adaptable. External support (US and Japanese firms and markets, European wind investment, global demand for chips) opened the door. 'Hybrid' means state-guided planning, private firms, city initiatives and outside demand working together. The influence ratings are my judgement, not measured: say so if asked. TAKEAWAY (1 min). STATE-GUIDED: the export zone (1966) was built and run by the government; Ten Major Construction created China Steel (1971, first furnace 1977), the CSBC shipyard (1973) and refinery capacity; the Nanzih park sits on former state-owned CPC refinery land and the city handled TSMC's approvals; the city led the Asia New Bay Area (from 2011). EXTERNALLY ENABLED: foreign (including American) firms in the export zone and the US market; Ørsted ordering foundations from Singda Harbor; Danish NKT's cable joint venture with Walsin Lihwa; Hon Hai and Nvidia's planned AI centre at the Software Park; global demand for containers, steel, ships and chips. Rooted locally: ASE, China Steel, city initiatives and citizen action. Next challenge: widen beyond one chip anchor and keep young people. UNCERTAIN, verify: Nvidia centre status (announced, not confirmed built); American firms in the export zone; the city's exact role in the TSMC approval. Invite questions.
